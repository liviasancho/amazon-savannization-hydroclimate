# Definition of the Arc of Deforestation

## Rationale

The Arc of Deforestation used in this study represents the recent spatial frontier of deforestation within the Brazilian Legal Amazon.

Rather than adopting a fixed historical Arc boundary, the study defined the region based on the spatial concentration of PRODES deforestation observed in 2024.

## Method

We intersected 2024 PRODES deforestation with municipalities within the Brazilian Legal Amazon.

Municipalities were ranked according to their contribution to total deforested area in 2024. We selected municipalities cumulatively accounting for approximately 75% of total deforestation.

The resulting selection comprised 82 municipalities and represented 469,986.33 ha of the total 626,154.54 ha of mapped 2024 deforestation (75.0592%).

Because some selected municipalities were spatially isolated from the main group, we excluded them. The final study region therefore consists of the spatially connected portion of the municipal set representing approximately 75% of 2024 deforestation.

We stored the resulting polygon in SIRGAS 2000 (EPSG:4674) and used it as the Arc of Deforestation mask throughout the climate-extreme analyses.
