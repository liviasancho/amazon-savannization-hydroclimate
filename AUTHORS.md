The contribution descriptions below complement the author-contribution statement provided in the manuscript and provide additional detail on the computational and analytical workflows represented in this repository.

## Repository contributions

The repository is being assembled and maintained centrally to preserve a consistent structure, provenance record, and reproducible workflow across the different analytical components of the study.

Analytical scripts, processing documentation, and data provenance are incorporated according to the contributions described above.

The repository currently documents workflows associated with:

- land-use and land-cover change;
- climate-extreme indices;
- SPEI;
- atmospheric blocking;
- blocking–SPEI relationships;
- the Arc of Deforestation;
- land-surface temperature.

Additional technical details will be incorporated as the remaining workflows are finalized.
